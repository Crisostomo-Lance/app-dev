# **Maleficent**: The Dark Fairy's Tale

## Plot Summary
*Maleficent* is a dark fantasy film that tells the untold story of the iconic villain from Disney's *Sleeping Beauty*. It explores her background, motivations, and eventual transformation into the wicked fairy. As a young fairy, Maleficent lives in a peaceful forest kingdom, but her life takes a dark turn when she is betrayed by the man she loves.

## Key Characters:
- **Maleficent**: Portrayed by Angelina Jolie, she is the main protagonist and antagonist of the story.
- **Princess Aurora**: The beautiful princess cursed by Maleficent, played by Elle Fanning.
- **Stefan**: The human king who betrays Maleficent, portrayed by Sharlto Copley.

## Famous Quote:
> *"I don't like your attitude, Princess."* — Maleficent
